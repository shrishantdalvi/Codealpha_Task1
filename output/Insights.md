- Overall, there is no strong relationship between labour participation rate and unemployment rate during COVID. This suggests that labour participation alone does not explain unemployment during the COVID period.

## 17. Top10 region with highest Estimated Employed during covid19
- High labour participation does not always mean more employment.
Tripura had the highest labour participation rate (61%), but it also had the highest unemployment rate (32.67%) and was not among the top estimated employed states. This shows that a high labour participation rate alone did not guarantee more employment during COVID.

- Higher estimated employment was generally associated with lower unemployment.
States such as Uttar Pradesh (2.858 crore), Maharashtra (2.006 crore), Tamil Nadu (1.344 crore), Bihar (1.290 crore), Madhya Pradesh (1.140 crore), and Rajasthan (1.004 crore) had the highest estimated employment. Except for Bihar (26.64%), most of these states were not among the regions with the highest unemployment, suggesting that larger employment levels were associated with relatively lower unemployment.

- Haryana is an exception.
Haryana had a high unemployment rate (31%) even though its labour participation rate was 41%. It was also not among the states with the highest estimated employment. This indicates that moderate labour participation did not prevent high unemployment.

- Bihar shows that high employment and high unemployment can exist together.
Bihar had one of the highest estimated employed populations (1.290 crore) but also recorded a high unemployment rate (26.64%). This suggests that a large workforce does not necessarily mean that enough jobs are available for everyone.

- Overall relationship between the three variables.
The comparison shows that estimated employed has a weak negative relationship with unemployment, while labour participation has no strong relationship with unemployment. During COVID, employment levels were influenced by multiple factors, and neither labour participation nor estimated employment alone could fully explain the unemployment rate.
